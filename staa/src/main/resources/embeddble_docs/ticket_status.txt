Understanding Support Ticket Statuses

When you contact support, a ticket is created to track your issue. Here is what each status means:

- Open: Your ticket has been received and is in the queue, waiting to be picked up by an agent.
- In Progress: An agent is actively working on your issue.
- Waiting on Customer: We have asked you a question or requested more information. The ticket will remain in this state until you respond.
- Escalated: Your issue has been passed to a specialized team or senior agent because it requires additional expertise.
- Resolved: The issue has been fixed or answered. The ticket will automatically close after 5 days if there is no further response.
- Closed: The ticket is complete and no further action is being taken. You can reopen a closed ticket within 30 days by replying to the original ticket email.

Response time expectations:
- Standard tickets: response within 24 business hours.
- Priority/urgent tickets (account access, billing issues): response within 4 business hours.
- Escalated tickets: response within 48 hours due to specialized review.

How to check your ticket status:
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to "Support" then "My Tickets".
3. Click on the ticket number to see the current status and full conversation history.

How to escalate a ticket yourself:
If you feel your issue needs urgent attention, reply to the ticket with "URGENT" in the subject line, or use the "Request Escalation" button on the ticket detail page.
