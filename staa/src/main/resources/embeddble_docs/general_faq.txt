General Account FAQ

How do I update my email address?
Go to Account Settings, then Profile, and click "Edit" next to your email address. You will need to verify the new email by clicking a confirmation link sent to it. Your old email will remain active until the new one is verified.

How do I change my shipping address?
Go to Account Settings, then Addresses. You can add a new address or edit an existing one. Note: changing your address will not affect orders that have already shipped.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Account Settings, then Subscriptions, and click "Cancel Subscription". Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds are issued for the remaining days.

How do I request a refund?
Refunds can be requested within 30 days of purchase from the Orders page by selecting "Request Refund" on the relevant order. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days and returned to the original payment method.

How do I contact a human agent instead of the AI assistant?
If the AI assistant cannot resolve your issue, type "talk to a human" or click "Escalate to Agent" at the bottom of the chat. A support ticket will be created and a human agent will follow up within the standard response time.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Account Settings, then Privacy, and click "Delete Account". This action is permanent and cannot be undone after 30 days. During the 30-day grace period, you can log in again to cancel the deletion.
