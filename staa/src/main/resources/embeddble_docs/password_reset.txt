How to Reset Your Password

If you forgot your password, follow these steps to reset it:

1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password".
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset link. The email may take up to 5 minutes to arrive.
4. Click the link in the email. It is valid for 30 minutes.
5. Enter a new password. Your password must be at least 8 characters long and include one number and one special character.
6. Confirm the new password and click "Reset Password".
7. You will be redirected to the login page. Sign in with your new password.

If you do not receive the reset email, check your spam folder. If it still does not arrive after 15 minutes, contact support and request a manual password reset.

Common issues:
- "Reset link expired": Request a new link from the Forgot Password page.
- "Email not recognized": Make sure you are using the email address you registered with. You may have used a different email or signed up via Google/Apple login.
- Account locked after 5 failed attempts: Wait 15 minutes or contact support to unlock immediately.
